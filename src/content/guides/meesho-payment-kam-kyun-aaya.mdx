₹1,000 ka saaman becha aur bank mein ₹640 aaye. Yeh sawaal har Meesho seller poochta hai. Jawab
ek deduction nahi hai — kai deductions ka stack hai, jisme se kuch waajib hain, aur ek aisa kharcha
hai jo supplier panel mein dikhta hi nahi.

## Meesho paisa bhejne se pehle kya kaatta hai

**Commission** — Meesho ka hissa. Yeh category aur price slab ke hisaab se badalta hai, isliye ek
hi price ke do product par alag-alag charge lag sakta hai. "Lagbhag 10%" maan kar chalna aadhe SKUs
par galat hoga.

**Shipping charge** — parcel ke weight aur distance par. Yeh sabse zyada chaunkata hai: ek halka
item jo desh ke doosre kone mein ja raha hai, usmein ek bhaari local order se zyada kharcha aa
sakta hai. Matlab ek jaisi do sale alag amount par settle ho sakti hain.

**GST aur TCS/TDS** — aapke GSTIN aur PAN par collect karke jama kiya jata hai. Yeh settlement se
nikla hua asli paisa hai, jo aap apni return file karke wapas claim karte hain, Meesho se nahi.

**Return aur RTO charges** — parcel wapas aaye — chahe customer ne return kiya ho ya delivery hi
na hui ho — shipping charge phir bhi lagta hai. Stock wapas milta hai, lekin hamesha bechne layak
haalat mein nahi.

**Ads spend** — agar aap campaign chalate hain toh yeh per order nahi, account level par kaata
jata hai. Yahi woh deduction hai jo profitable dikhne wali product list ko aksar loss mein badal
deta hai, kyunki kisi ek order par yeh kahin nazar nahi aata.

**Compensation aur claims** — doosri taraf, kho gaye ya damage hue parcels ke liye Meesho paisa
wapas bhi jodta hai. Yeh apni alag rows mein aata hai.

Jo bachta hai woh hai **Final Settlement Amount**, aur hisaab wahin se shuru karna chahiye. Upar ke
saare deductions us number ke andar pehle se hain — unhe dobara ghatana double counting hai.

## Jo Meesho ko dikhta hi nahi

Ab woh hissa jahan supplier panel kuch madad nahi kar sakta. Meesho bata sakta hai ki usne aapko
kitna diya. Profit nahi bata sakta, kyunki yeh sab usko dikhta hi nahi:

- har item banane ya kharidne ki cost
- packing material, tape aur filler
- label, ink aur printer paper
- rent, bijli aur internet
- salary — apni bhi
- return aaya hua stock jo ab bik nahi sakta

Panel ka dashboard revenue report hai jo profit report ke kapde pehne hue hai. Final Settlement
Amount aur aapke haath mein sach mein bachne wale paise ke beech ka farak hi asli sawaal hai — aur
[profit calculator](/hi/tools/meesho-profit-calculator) usi farak ko naapne ke liye banaya gaya hai.

## Hisaab match kyun nahi hota

Teen cheezein log aksar miss kar dete hain:

1. **Dates payment ki hain, order ki nahi.** Ek hafte ki payment file un orders ko settle karti
   hai jo kai hafte pehle aaye the — delivery aur return window ke baad. Isliye ek hi period ke
   orders se milane par hisaab kabhi nahi baithega.
2. **Kuch orders do rows mein settle hote hain.** Pehli row, phir baad ka adjustment — jiska
   status aksar blank hota hai. Dono real hain. Status par filter lagate hi doosri chup-chaap
   gayab ho jati hai.
3. **RTO cancel order nahi hai.** Woh ek poora shipping kaam hai jo aapke hi pate par khatam hua.
   Shipping ka kharcha dono taraf se gaya, aur packing bhi gayi.

## Asli margin kaise nikalein

Supplier panel mein: **Payments → Download → Payments to Date**, phir apni dates chunein. Sirf
wahi file per-order settlement rows leke aati hai. Us button ke peeche baaki chaar downloads (GST
Report, Tax Invoice, Supplier Tax Invoice, Outstanding Payments) mein woh rows hoti hi nahi,
isliye unse yeh kaam nahi ho sakta.

Phir teen cheezein aap jodte hain, jo sirf aapko pata hain:

- **Per-SKU cost** — item kharidne ya banane ka kharcha, aur uske pack karne ka.
- **Return par nuksan** — RTO, customer return ya transit mein kho gaye parcel par aap sach mein
  kitna write off karte hain. Na 0%, na poora 100%.
- **Monthly kharche** — rent, bijli, salary. Yeh file ke poore period par bantte hain, kisi ek
  order par nahi — kyunki koi ek order aapka rent paida nahi karta.

Jo number nikalta hai woh aksar umeed se kam hota hai, aur mahine bhar mein aapke bank balance ke
kaafi kareeb hota hai. Yahi is hisaab ka matlab hai.

## Yaad rakhne wali baatein

- Settlement mein commission, shipping, GST, TCS/TDS, return aur ads pehle se kat chuke hain.
- Phir bhi woh profit nahi hai — aapke apne kharche abhi ghatne baaki hain.
- Payment dates order dates nahi hain, isliye hafte-war milan kabhi nahi hoga.
- Ads account par lagte hain, order par nahi — poore mahine ke hisaab se dekhein.
- Per order profitable dikhne wala product bhi return aur ads ke baad loss de sakta hai.
